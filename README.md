## 📖 项目简介 | Project Overview

### 中文
《汉宫春晓图》是中国重彩仕女第一长卷，位列“中国十大传世名画”之一。画作以极其华丽的笔墨描绘了汉宫内外的景致和初春时节宫闱仕女的生活情景。用笔清劲，设色鲜妍，将人物与背景和谐统一，情节充满趣味，展现了高超的艺术技巧与巧于构思的艺术才能。

在弘扬中华优秀传统文化与数字媒介快速发展的时代背景下，本项目将数字技术融入传统文化视域，通过将《汉宫春晓图》引入互动游戏创作，探索密室逃脱解谜游戏开发的新思路。游戏依据名画的绘画内容、人物造型、构图设色与笔法特色，重构了《汉宫春晓图》的空间之境、文化之境与艺术之境，旨在提升受众对中国传统人物画的认知与理解，促进数字时代下的文化认同。

### English
*Spring Morning in the Han Palace* is the premier long scroll of Chinese noblewomen painted in heavy color and is renowned as one of the "Ten Famous Paintings Handed Down in China." The painting depicts the vibrant life of court ladies and the scenery inside and outside the Han Palace in early spring with gorgeous ink, clear brushwork, and vivid colors. 

Against the backdrop of promoting fine traditional culture and digital media expansion, this project integrates digital technology into traditional art perspectives by introducing *Spring Morning in the Han Palace* into game design. By analyzing the painting's artistic features—such as character modeling, composition, color setting, and brushwork—the game reconstructs its spatial, cultural, and artistic environments. It explores a novel approach to room escape puzzle games while improving public engagement and cultural identity in the digital media era.

## 🎮 游戏关卡设计 | Level Architecture

本项目共包含两大核心密室关卡 / The game consists of two main secret room escape levels:

1. **宫室内关卡：《无边乐事趁春明》**  
   * **Indoor Level: "Endless Joys in Spring"**  
   依托宫室内部建筑与场景，结合仕女日常活动与室内道具，设计一系列探索与机关解谜。  
   Focuses on interior architecture and court ladies' daily activities, featuring interactive mechanisms and clue solving.

2. **宫室外关卡：《丹青图画春风里》**  
   * **Outdoor Level: "Danqing Painting in the Spring Breeze"**  
   延伸至宫廷园林与自然景致，融入画卷的构图意境，完成跨场景的情节互动与线索解密。  
   Extends to palace gardens and natural landscapes, integrating artistic composition with cross-scene plot progression.
